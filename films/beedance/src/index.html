A bee just found
a field of flowers.
Now she has to tell
the others.
in the dark
So she dances.
A figure of eight.
over and over
The way she tilts…
…is the way
to the flowers.
Straight up means:
fly toward the sun.
The longer
she waggles…
…the further you go.
Worked out by
Karl von Frisch
Nobel Prize · 1973
A bee drew a map.
With her body.
In the dark.
