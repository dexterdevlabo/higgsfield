Neither verified archival nor code-drawn. Record the model, prompt and seed for every image
so the film's provenance stays auditable, the same standard the other films in `films/` hold.

Currently `assets/generated/` holds **drawn placeholders**, made locally with ImageMagick at
zero cost, so the film renders and can be judged before anything is bought.

## On-screen disclosure

Generated illustration is not presented as photography and contains no claim of its own —
it establishes place only. Every factual statement is carried by code-drawn diagrams and the
voiceover, all sourced in `PROJECT.md`. No disclosure caption is therefore required, but if
the series later uses generated imagery to depict a real event or person, it must be labelled.

## Code-drawn, zero cost

Honeycomb grids, the bee, the figure-of-eight path, the angle diagram, the sun and rays, the
distance bar, every title, caption and chip, the hive label, all transitions. Plus the entire
sound bed, synthesised in `src/audio.py`.
