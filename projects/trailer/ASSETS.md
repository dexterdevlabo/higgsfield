## Notes on on-screen claims
On-screen dates reproduce the **source institution's own dating**, not PDR's and not a guess:
"ca. 1830–33" for A03 follows the LoC record "[between 1830 and 1833]". No image is captioned
as a place, person or event beyond what its catalogue record states. Nothing is colourised; no
recreations or generated imagery appear, so no recreation labelling is required.
